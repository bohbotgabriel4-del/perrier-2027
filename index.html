Behind the pitch: the prompt

Our strategy is about showing the raw work, so we show ours too. We built this pitch with an AI assistant (Claude). Here is the brief we gave it, translated and shortened:

Read all our Strategic Marketing course sessions first, then confirm you understood the course and the brief (session 2). We are two Marketing Directors of a brand inside a large group, pitching our 2027 strategy to the Global CMO, Nadia: 10 minutes + 5 minutes of Q&A, 10 to 20 slides. Brand: Perrier. Cover where the brand stands in 2025–26, what changes in 2027 and why, two priority markets (ideal customer, segmentation, channels and expected results, content ideas, a realistic budget, loyalty) and 4 to 5 KPIs with realistic targets. Deliver: (1) a rock-solid deck in English, using real facts and a design inspired by Perrier; (2) an English report in the same colours; (3) a speaker split between Gabriel and Elie, slide by slide, the hardest slides for Elie; (4) a French prep guide that ends with likely jury questions, answered in English with the French translation. Then we iterated: a green-to-blue design, a video transition, a live QR code that opens this page.
  • Every number was checked against a dated source (list below).
  • We chose the brand, the two markets and the final ask ourselves, and rewrote the script in our own words.
  • The AI drafted; we decided. Proof, not polish, applies to us too.

Ask us anything

Isn’t a QR code on a bottle a gimmick?

Only if it shows marketing. Ours shows raw data, including bad results. A bad result published first is what makes the system credible.

Why the UK and not another market?

Maison Perrier launched there in February 2026, the low and no-alcohol market is about £413m, and the gap is distribution in pubs. That is a problem a budget can solve.

What if a test is bad?

We publish it first, with what we are doing about it. Transparency that only shows good news is not transparency.

Isn’t +3% sales modest?

On purpose. After a year of decline, getting back to growth is the realistic first target; trust is the leading indicator.
